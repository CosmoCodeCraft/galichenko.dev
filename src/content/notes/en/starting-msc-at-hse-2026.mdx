On 25 August 2026, I officially became a master's student at HSE University.

I joined the [MSc in System and Software Engineering at HSE University](https://www.hse.ru/en/ma/se/) at the Faculty of Computer Science on a state-funded place.

My bachelor's background was in control systems, but by graduation much of the engineering work I found most interesting was already happening at the boundaries between components: Linux infrastructure, communication between devices and software, service integration and system architecture.

The master's programme is a way to move that interest further into software engineering.

My current direction is primarily DevOps, platform engineering and systems integration. In the [SIRD team project](/projects/sird), that means working with containerized services, networking and PostgreSQL, while CI/CD, Kubernetes, Helm, monitoring and tracing form the next infrastructure layer.

The move to HSE is therefore less a complete change of field than a change of abstraction level: from making software and hardware work together to making software components and infrastructure work together reliably.
