On 8 July 2026, I received my bachelor's diploma from Moscow Polytechnic University.

I graduated with honours from the Electronic Control Systems profile within Control in Technical Systems.

The degree ended up being much broader for me than the programme title suggests. During those four years I worked with embedded control, automation, Linux infrastructure, engineering research, computer vision and project leadership.

[City Farm](/projects/city-farm) was the first project where many of those areas started to connect. It later led to my bachelor's thesis on monitoring aeroponic irrigation with computer vision and to the research work documented in [Computer Vision for Aeroponic Irrigation Monitoring](/projects/vision).

The graduation ceremony on 8 July was the first of the university's 2026 graduation ceremonies and included honours graduates and students recognised for active university involvement.

For me, the diploma closed one stage rather than the whole direction. The next step was moving from control systems toward software and systems engineering at HSE University.
