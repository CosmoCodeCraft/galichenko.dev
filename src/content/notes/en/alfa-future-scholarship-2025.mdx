In 2025, I became a winner of the Alfa-Future Scholarships programme by Alfa-Bank.

The programme selected students based on their academic and professional achievements and combined financial support with access to the wider Alfa-Future community and its activities.

I received my winner's certificate at Moscow Polytechnic on 22 October, during the university's First-Year Student festival.

By that point I was in the final year of my bachelor's degree, working on my thesis and continuing research and project activity.

For me, the scholarship was useful external recognition of work that had accumulated over several years across engineering projects, research and university activities.
